* **VERIFY reads a FIX plan as a fix-plan.** `validate_verify.py` asked every plan for `## Block N` and `test_x` names, so a fix-plan, which has neither, was refused however complete its verdict was. With `--tier FIX`, or a ticket that resolves to `fix-{ticket}.md`, F-VER-02 holds the report to every numbered step under `## Solution — steps` (`Step N`) and F-VER-06 to every bullet under `## Tests`, by its `test_x` name or, when only described, by its position (`Test N`); a step or test missing or marked failing is a ❌ that names it, and a plan with no steps or no tests is refused. On a FIX with no PRD, F-VER-01 says the fix-plan is the contract; a PRD that exists is checked as before. FEATURE is checked as before, and the independent review by `daw-module-verifier` is still owed for every tier, FIX included. The fix is Mateo's (@mdiloreto), from #57.
